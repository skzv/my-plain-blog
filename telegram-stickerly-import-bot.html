A Telegram Bot for Importing Sticker.ly Packs

By Sasha Kuznetsov
2023-04-05
Updated 2026-11-25

Import animated Sticker.ly packs into Telegram with ease

Sticker.ly is a popular sticker pack creation and management app for Android and iPhone devices with over 100 million downloads on the Google Play store alone. It supports importing sticker packs into iMessage, WhatsApp, and Telegram. However, the ability to import animated sticker packs is limited to WhatsApp only.

As an avid Telegram user and evangelist, I often encourage my friends to join the platform to communicate with me. Many of them are frustrated by the lack of access to the rich sticker library available on other platforms like WhatsApp. I promised to fix this, and I finally did. Introducing: the Sticker.ly Sticker Pack Import Bot (also listed with quick instructions at stickers.skz.dev).

How It Works

To use the bot, open @StickerlyImportBot in Telegram and send a Sticker.ly pack URL, such as https://sticker.ly/s/HBBCR8. The bot fetches the pack, converts the animated stickers into a video-sticker format compatible with Telegram’s Bot API requirements, registers the pack, and replies with a direct link to add the animated sticker pack to your Telegram library.

Animated vs. Static Packs

Although the bot also supports importing static sticker packs, I recommend against using it for static packs, since Sticker.ly and Telegram already support exporting static packs natively out of the box. The bot is most useful for animated packs that Sticker.ly restricts to WhatsApp.

Troubleshooting and Limits

The bot is free to use at t.me/StickerlyImportBot and stickers.skz.dev. Because conversion depends on upstream pack links and Telegram’s sticker rate limits, occasional failures can occur on very large packs. If a sticker pack only partially uploads, follow the in-bot instructions to remove the incomplete pack and send the Sticker.ly URL again.
